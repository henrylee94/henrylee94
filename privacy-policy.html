Privacy Policy — hermes-mcp

Last updated: 2026-10-19

hermes-mcp is a personal, single-user application that connects to Google Workspace APIs (Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Calendar, Gmail read-only, Tasks) on behalf of its owner.

Data accessed

  • Google Drive / Docs / Sheets / Slides / Calendar / Tasks: read and write, for personal document and schedule management.
  • Gmail: read-only (message list and content), used to summarize the owner's own inbox.

Data usage

  • Data is processed locally on the owner's machine only.
  • No data is sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising technologies are used.

Data retention

Only OAuth credentials are stored locally, on the owner's device. They remain active until revoked by the owner.

Contact

Revoking access

You can revoke this app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
